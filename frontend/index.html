Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Purpose and Acceptance

Reimbursement System is an enterprise expense governance and ledger management platform. By accessing this platform, users represent that they are authorized agents of their organization and agree to abide by internal corporate procurement policies and these terms.

2. Fiduciary Responsibility and Submissions

All expense claims submitted through this system must represent authentic, legitimate business expenditures incurred in the course of company business. Falsification of receipts or duplicate submissions constitutes grounds for audit, disciplinary measures, and revocation of access.

3. Administrative Audit and Approvals

Organization administrators retain full audit privileges to review, approve, reject, or request amendments to all submitted documentation. All decisions recorded in the audit log are immutable for accounting compliance purposes.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Data Collection Scope

Reimbursement System processes employee identity data (name, company email, organizational role) and financial voucher metadata (amounts, currencies, dates, merchant details, and uploaded receipt images) solely for corporate accounting and reimbursement fulfillment.

2. Data Retention and Safeguards

Financial records and voucher documents are stored with strict role-based access control. Expense details are accessible only by the submitter and authorized company administrators.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Personal and corporate financial data is never rented or monetized. External exchange rate calculations are executed via secure programmatic currency conversion endpoints without transmitting user-identifying information.
